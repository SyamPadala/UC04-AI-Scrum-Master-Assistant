Introducing

The Scrum Assistant

The daily stand-up, done for you.
Inside Microsoft Teams, the tool your people already use.

The problem

Stand-ups take more time than they should

Every day, Scrum Masters spend a big part of their time on admin instead of helping their team.

Chasing people

Reminding everyone, one by one, every single day.

Copying updates

Typing what people said into a tracker by hand.

Hearing about problems late

A blocked person can wait hours before anyone knows.

Writing reports

Stakeholders want a clear picture, so someone builds it every evening.

The solution

A helper in Teams that runs the stand-up

It asks, listens, writes it down, raises problems and reports. Everyone gets something out of it.

Team members

  • Reply in their own words, in a chat
  • No forms, no spreadsheets
  • Blockers reach help faster

Scrum Masters

  • No chasing, no copying
  • Told about blockers straight away
  • Time back to coach the team

Stakeholders

  • A clear summary every day
  • In Teams and by email
  • See progress and risks early
How it works

A normal day with the assistant

Morning
It asks. Everyone gets a friendly message at stand-up time.
When they reply
It understands. People answer in plain words. It works out what's done, what's in progress and what's stuck.
Seconds later
It records. The update is saved to the team's tracker, against the right piece of work.
If stuck
It raises the flag. The Scrum Master hears about a blocker straight away.
2 hours later
It nudges. Only people who haven't replied get a reminder.
Evening
It reports. Stakeholders get the summary, and the day is closed.

What it can do

Key features

Understands plain language

No templates. People write the way they talk.

Asks when unsure

If an update is unclear, it offers simple buttons instead of guessing.

Instant blocker alerts

The Scrum Master knows within seconds, not at the end of the day.

Smart reminders

Only people who haven't replied get a nudge.

Daily summary

Progress against the sprint goal, blockers and work at risk, in Teams and email.

Participation tracking

Shows who is replying and quietly flags people who keep missing.

Scrum Master stays in control

Approves updates on other people's work, can pause or reopen the day.

Many teams, one tool

Each team has its own times, people, tracker and stakeholders.

Built for the whole organisation

Many teams, one simple control page

Every team works its own way

One assistant serves all teams. Each team gets its own:

  • stand-up and report times, and time zone
  • people and Scrum Master
  • tracker and Jira project
  • stakeholders who get its report

One Scrum Master can look after several teams.

The admin page: everything in one place

A web page, opened with your normal Microsoft sign-in. No technical skills needed.

  • Set up a new team: people, times, tracker, stakeholders
  • Add or remove a person by email; their Teams and Jira access is handled for them
  • Change times, stakeholders or tracker at any time
  • Readiness check shows what's missing before going live
  • See today's activity, and run any step on demand
Where we are

Live today with two teams, and safe by design

What's working now

  • Running every day for two scrum teams
  • Reads updates correctly about 94% of the time in our tests
  • Stand-up, blockers, reminders and the daily report all automatic
